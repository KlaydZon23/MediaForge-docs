Media Forge

A private tool for creating and publishing short videos to YouTube channels owned by the operator.

What it does

Media Forge is a desktop application that runs on the operator's own computer. It helps a single operator manage the short-form video (YouTube Shorts) channels they own:

  • cuts short clips from video material the operator is licensed to use (for example brand material provided under a creator campaign);
  • uploads those clips to the operator's own YouTube channels with a title and description;
  • updates the description, keywords, country and language of the operator's own channels;
  • reads public statistics (views, likes) of the operator's own videos to show them in a dashboard.

Google and YouTube permissions

The app asks the channel owner to sign in with Google and requests only these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload — to upload the operator's own videos;
  • youtube.readonly — to read the operator's own channel and video statistics;
  • youtube.force-ssl — to update the operator's own channel description and keywords.

The app does not read private data of other people, does not post comments, does not delete content, and does not sell or share YouTube data with anyone.

Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. It is used only by its operator on channels the operator owns. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
