Privacy Policy

Media Forge · last updated: 4 October 2026

1. Who we are

Media Forge is a private desktop tool operated by one person on their own computer. This policy explains what data the app handles when the operator connects their own YouTube channels through Google sign-in.

2. Data we access

With the channel owner's consent, the app accesses through the YouTube API:

  • basic information about the owner's own channel (title, description, ID);
  • statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes);
  • the ability to upload videos and edit the channel description and keywords on the owner's own channel.

We do not access data of other users.

3. How data is stored

All data, including the Google sign-in tokens, is stored only in files on the operator's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server operated by us, because we do not operate a server. Data is not sold, rented, shared or disclosed to third parties.

4. How data is used

Data is used only to operate the features described on the home page: publishing the owner's videos, updating the owner's channel details and showing the owner's statistics. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Media Forge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the operator's computer until the operator deletes it. Disconnecting a channel in the app removes its stored tokens. The owner can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of anything related to you, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
